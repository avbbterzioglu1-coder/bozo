Our Practice Areas

We provide legal consultancy and litigation/enforcement services in the following areas.

Commercial Law

We provide consultancy and litigation/enforcement services in disputes arising from legal relationships between commercial enterprises.

  • Drafting and review of commercial contracts
  • Commercial receivables and compensation disputes
  • Litigation and enforcement proceedings before commercial courts

Corporate Law

We provide legal consultancy throughout the entire life cycle of a company, from incorporation to the termination of shareholder relationships.

  • Company incorporation and articles of association
  • Mergers, acquisitions and share transfer transactions
  • Shareholder and board of directors disputes
  • Consultancy on general assembly procedures

Contract Law

We provide consultancy in drafting contracts that secure the rights and obligations of the parties in commercial and private-law relationships, and in resolving related disputes.

  • Drafting and negotiation of contract drafts
  • Resolution of disputes arising from contracts
  • Consultancy on withdrawal, termination and penalty clauses

Concordat

We provide legal consultancy to both debtors and creditors in concordat proceedings, which allow a debtor in financial difficulty to restructure its debts through a court-supervised agreement with creditors.

  • Consultancy during preparation of the concordat pre-project
  • Filing the concordat request with the commercial court
  • Representing the debtor during the interim and final moratorium periods
  • Creditor-side proceedings for claim admission and ranking objections

Labor and Social Security Law

We provide legal consultancy and litigation services to employees or employers in disputes arising from the employment relationship and social security legislation.

  • Employment claims such as severance and notice pay, overtime and annual leave
  • Reinstatement proceedings following termination of employment
  • Representation in the mandatory mediation process
  • Disputes arising from service determination and social security legislation

Enforcement and Bankruptcy Law

We handle enforcement and bankruptcy proceedings both to collect claims and to defend against enforcement proceedings brought against our clients.

  • Initiating enforcement proceedings with or without a judgment
  • Objections to enforcement, and actions to cancel or lift such objections
  • Actions for claims of ownership and cancellation of transactions

Compensation Law

We pursue legal proceedings for the recovery of material and non-material damages arising from traffic accidents and similar incidents.

  • Material and non-material damages claims arising from traffic accidents
  • Subrogation claims against insurance companies

Criminal Law

We act as defense counsel for suspects and defendants, and as counsel for victims and complainants, during investigation and prosecution.

  • Defense counsel during the investigation stage
  • Defense counsel during the prosecution stage
  • Counsel for victims and complainants

Sports Law

We provide consultancy and litigation/arbitration services in legal disputes arising from professional and amateur sporting activities.

  • Drafting and review of player and club contracts
  • Transfer and licensing disputes
  • Representation in sports arbitration proceedings
  • Consultancy on disciplinary proceedings

International Trade Law & Arbitration

We provide legal consultancy in the resolution of disputes arising from cross-border commercial relationships and in arbitration proceedings.

  • Drafting of international sale and distribution agreements
  • Representation in international arbitration proceedings
  • Recognition and enforcement of foreign court judgments and arbitral awards
  • Resolution of cross-border commercial disputes

Construction and Zoning Law

We provide legal consultancy at every stage of construction projects and in disputes arising from zoning legislation.

  • Drafting of construction and works contracts
  • Disputes arising from build-to-share (flat-for-land) construction contracts
  • Actions against zoning plans and administrative acts
  • Compensation claims arising from defective work and delay

Intellectual Property Law

We provide consultancy on the protection of trademark, patent, design and copyright rights, and in disputes arising from such rights.

  • Consultancy on trademark and patent registration procedures
  • Actions to prevent infringement of trademark and design rights
  • Drafting of licensing and assignment agreements
  • Unfair competition actions

Tax Law

We handle administrative applications and litigation in tax disputes, and provide consultancy on tax legislation.

  • Settlement and litigation proceedings in tax disputes
  • Objections to and actions for annulment of tax penalties
  • Consultancy during tax audit proceedings

Data Protection Law

We provide consultancy on compliance processes and disputes under Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data.

  • Compliance consultancy on data controller and data processor obligations
  • Drafting of privacy notices and explicit consent processes
  • Applications and complaints before the Personal Data Protection Board
  • Consultancy on data breach notification procedures

Information Technology Law

We provide consultancy and dispute resolution services in the legal processes of technology companies and digital platforms.

  • Drafting of software licensing and service agreements
  • Compliance consultancy on e-commerce legislation
  • Consultancy on cybersecurity and data breach processes
  • Disputes arising from electronic communications legislation

About Us

We provide legal consultancy and litigation services in commercial law, corporate law and concordat proceedings, as well as labor law, enforcement and bankruptcy law, compensation law, criminal law and other areas.

We assess every file individually, on its own specific facts, and keep our clients regularly informed at each stage of the process. Client information and documents are kept in strict confidence under attorney-client privilege.

We accompany parties with different needs — from individual clients to commercial enterprises — with the same care from the beginning to the end of the process. During consultations, we place particular importance on listening carefully to the client's situation and explaining the available options and the legal process in clear terms. The timely provision of requested information and documents helps the process move forward smoothly. We regard being accessible to our clients' questions at every stage as a requirement of our profession.
